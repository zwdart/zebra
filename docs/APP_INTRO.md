## 二、功能介绍文案

**你的随身效率工具箱,一个应用全搞定。**

**🔌 连接管理** — 集中管理你的 SSH 服务器连接,支持密码与密钥登录,
快速连接、随手编辑,常用服务器一目了然。

**🖥️ 远程终端** — 内置终端模拟器,随时随地执行远程命令,操作流畅不卡顿。

**📁 文件传输 (SFTP)** — 拖拽即可上传下载文件,远程文件管理像本地一样简单。

**📊 服务器监控** — 实时查看 CPU、内存等运行状态,服务器健康尽在掌握。

**📰 RSS 订阅** — 聚合你关注的资讯源,随时随地阅读,不错过任何更新。

**📝 日记** — 随手记录想法与日常,支持导出,你的私人空间。

**🔳 二维码工具** — 一键生成、识别二维码,本地处理,数据不出设备。

**🔒 安全可靠** — 数据本地存储并加密,不上传云端,隐私由你掌控。

**📱 多端通用** — 桌面、手机全平台支持,一处配置,处处可用。

### Feature Introduction

**Your all-in-one productivity toolbox. One app, everything you need.**

**🔌 Connection Manager** — Manage all your SSH server connections in one
place. Password or key-based login, quick connect, easy editing — your
frequently used servers are always at hand.

**🖥️ Remote Terminal** — A built-in terminal emulator lets you run remote
commands anytime, smoothly and responsively.

**📁 File Transfer (SFTP)** — Drag and drop to upload or download files.
Remote file management as easy as working locally.

**📊 Server Monitor** — Watch CPU, memory, and other metrics in real time
to keep your servers healthy.

**📰 RSS Feeds** — Aggregate the news sources you follow and read them
anywhere, so you never miss an update.

**📝 Diary** — Jot down ideas and everyday moments, with export support.
Your private space.

**🔳 QR Code Tool** — Generate and scan QR codes in one tap, processed
locally — your data never leaves your device.

**🔒 Secure & Private** — Data is stored locally and encrypted, never
uploaded to the cloud. Your privacy, your control.

**📱 Cross-Platform** — Works on desktop and mobile. Configure once,
use it everywhere.

### Short Version (English)

Zebra SSH is a cross-platform productivity toolbox: manage all your SSH
connections in one place, with a built-in terminal, SFTP file transfer,
and server monitoring; plus RSS feeds, diary, and QR code tools. Data is
stored locally and encrypted — never uploaded to the cloud. Available on
desktop and mobile. One app, everywhere.
